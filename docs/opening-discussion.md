# Try Sold Vehicle Page Planner and share what would make it more useful

This free browser tool is for dealership owners and stock teams. Work through a sold-stock page and prepare a sensible next-step brief for your website supplier.

[Open the tool](https://seo-for-car-dealerships.github.io/sold-stock-decision-tool/). No coding knowledge, installation or account is needed. Start with the fictional example, or fill in the labelled fields with your own information. A page decision to review, with plain instructions for your website provider.

Feedback about unclear questions, missing practical options or mobile usability is welcome here. Please use fictional information in public examples and keep customer or confidential details out of this discussion.

This creates a recommendation for human review. It does not inspect traffic, change pages or implement redirects.

Maintained by [SEO for Car Dealerships](https://seoforcardealerships.co.uk/), operated by JW Digital Services Ltd. The original developer helpers are still available in the repository, but they are not needed to use the browser tool.
